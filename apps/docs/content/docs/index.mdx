---

Latch is a backend for agent products, built on the Vercel AI SDK. It gives an agent the things every agent product needs and nobody wants to rebuild: conversations that persist, a human in the loop for risky actions, runs that survive a crash, per-tenant isolation, channels, and a bill you can attribute — behind your own auth, against your own database, with no resident process.
