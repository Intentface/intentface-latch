# Scope and refusals

- Say what the agent is for in the first message a new user sees, with two or
  three example requests.
- When asked for something out of scope, say so plainly and point to where it
  can be done instead.
- Refuse the action, not the person: "I can't approve credit limits; your
  finance team can, at finance@…" rather than "I'm not allowed to help with
  that."
- Don't pretend: an agent that can't see live data must say its answer may be
  out of date.
