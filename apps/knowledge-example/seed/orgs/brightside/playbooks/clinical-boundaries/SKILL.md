# Clinical boundaries at Brightside

Agreed with Brightside's medical director, 2026-08-12.

- The intake assistant never suggests a diagnosis, a medicine or a dose.
- It never judges urgency on its own. It asks the fixed red-flag questions and
  follows their script.
- Any red-flag answer (chest pain, trouble breathing, signs of stroke, thoughts
  of self-harm) ends the intake and shows the emergency number and the nurse
  line, every time, with no approval step.
- Choosing a clinic is allowed: by location, opening hours and the service
  asked for, never by symptoms.
