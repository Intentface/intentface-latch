# Intake interviews, August 2026

Eight patients, five over 65.

- Several did not understand why the assistant asked about chest pain when
  they wanted a vaccination. They felt accused of being ill.
- Older patients wanted to know a person could take over, and how.
- Two abandoned the booking when asked to type their symptoms.

Proposal from the design team: explain why the red-flag questions are asked,
in one sentence, before asking them.
