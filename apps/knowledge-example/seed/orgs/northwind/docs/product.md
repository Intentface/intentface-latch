# Dispatch console

Northwind Freight moves part loads between the Nordics and the Baltics.
Twelve dispatchers in Tampere and Tallinn use the dispatch console all day.

The rebooking agent watches delayed shipments and proposes a new carrier or
slot. It went live for the Tallinn team in August 2026 and for Tampere in
September.

Volumes: about 900 shipments a day, 4% delayed, about a third of those
rebooked.
