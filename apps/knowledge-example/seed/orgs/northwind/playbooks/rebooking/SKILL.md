# Rebooking at Northwind

Agreed with Northwind's head of dispatch, 2026-09-30. This overrides the general
approvals practice where they differ.

- **Every rebooking needs a dispatcher's approval**, whatever its cost. After
  the September incident, Northwind has no auto-approve threshold for
  rebookings. Revisit in January 2027.
- Rebooking to a different carrier also needs the carrier's slot confirmed
  before the approval is asked.
- Never rebook the same shipment twice within 24 hours without a person
  starting it.
- Cancelling a shipment is out of scope for the agent entirely.
